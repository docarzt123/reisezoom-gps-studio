v0.9.648 2 September 2026 Current

The arrow sits still, the zoom stays put.

➤ The direction arrow no longer twitches. It took its heading from a single leg — with one-second recording that is one to three metres, exactly the range in which GPS noise lives. It now reads the heading from a longer stretch of track: on a real mountain tour the jump between frames drops from 22.6° to 3.5°. How steady it should be is up to you — the “Arrow steadiness” slider appears as soon as you pick the arrow shape.
  • Dragging the playhead no longer sends the camera off. Without camera keyframes and without “camera follows track”, the preview now shows the overview while dragging too — before it stuck to the point although the same project looked right during playback.
  • Moving into the library shows itself — with progress and the promise that nothing is deleted. And it copies in parallel: with tours on a network drive that is the difference between minutes and a quarter of an hour.
  • The outlier threshold for speed colouring can be set (“Red from … km/h”). Left empty, everything stays as before.
  • “Fill gaps as” suggests the mode of travel from the speed — with a visible reason, changeable with one click.
  • Deleting several projects could bring one back. They are now removed in one go, and the message counts what really went.
  • The watermark was missing when a project was opened and also disappeared with the stats overlays. Resizing moved it out of its corner — now its centre stays put.
  • Unreadable files show their folder and can be opened in Finder before you decide about them.
  • The dot size slider now applies to all tours, not just the main one. And the header says when more tours belong to the view.

v0.9.646 2 September 2026

Checked again: five things that were only almost right.

⌚ Recording with a watch did not give you a real backup copy. For a Garmin or Suunto file the raw recording was stored in the library unchanged — GPS Studio could no longer read it there. From now on it is converted when taken in, and anything already stored wrongly is repaired once at the next start — from what is stored. You do not need the original file for that.
  • Elevation gain is the same everywhere. Archive, inspector and elevation animator showed three numbers for the same tour. The archive’s calculation now applies everywhere — smoothed, 3-metre threshold, per leg.
  • A project can be set to a different version (🎬 on the version), and single versions can be exported (⬇) or deleted (🗑). That was promised but had no button.
  • Compressed tours (.gpx.gz) are read — Strava exports and many loggers deliver exactly that.
  • Missing map images are fetched automatically again, not only on demand.
  • “Find duplicates” now says what it does: these are your files, they go to your system’s trash, and the tour stays — the library keeps its own copy. That copy is no longer offered for removal.
  • The preview image in the detail column was sometimes missing although it existed.
v0.9.645 2 September 2026

The cloud now mirrors your library.

☁️ The cloud is a copy of your library. Previously GPS Studio uploaded a separate package per tour containing the directory, collections, projects and photos — a second data model beside the real one. Now it simply uploads what is in your library: the tours with all their versions, your projects, and everything you entered yourself. Encrypted before it leaves your machine.
  • “Load library from the cloud” sets up a second computer in one go — tours, projects, collections, tags. Existing files are not overwritten.
  • If the database is lost, the library is enough. The tours are taken back into the archive from it.
  • “Remove unknown” discards whatever does not belong to your library. Never automatically — with two computers, “unknown here” usually means “from the other machine”.
  • Without a cloud set up, nothing happens. No network, no keychain.
  • Not uploaded: the archive database (rebuilt), preview and map images (from Mapbox) and your photos.
v0.9.640 2 September 2026

Your tours now have a home.

📚 The library. GPS Studio now keeps your tours in one place that you choose — with its own copy of every tour. Watched folders are only the source: tidy up out there and your tour stays complete. On first start the app asks in three steps where the library should live; your existing data moves there by itself (backed up first, and the old state is only renamed, never deleted).
✋ GPS Studio never writes to your files any more. Healing a tour creates a new version in your library — your original file stays untouched, byte for byte. If you need the healed file outside the app, you export it deliberately.
🎯 One card per tour, not per file. The same route as a Komoot download, an app import and a healed copy used to appear three times. Now it is one tour, marked „V3" (three versions) and „2×" (from two files). Tags, favourite and your own name belong to the tour and survive a healing.
  • Pick any version. A project stays on its version — nothing shifts when you heal later. You can move it to another version at any time, backwards too.
  • Opening no longer creates a project. A project appears once you actually change something. No more dozens of empty cards called „Standard".
  • Deleting speaks up. A tour used by projects does not vanish silently — you see which ones and decide.
  • Inspector: „Colour by speed" stopped working on tours above 2,000 points — fixed.
  • The cloud archive is paused while the rebuild runs. Anything uploaded stays untouched; it will come back.

v0.9.554 27 August 2026

Repair the track without starting over.

🔁 Carry your work over to the healed track. If you had already built something in the animator and then noticed the track was off, repairing it used to cost you everything: to the program, a healed track is a new tour. Now the inspector asks once after saving — and brings the animator, tour map, geotagger and elevation animator along, photos and signs included. The old tour stays exactly as it was.

One caveat belongs with it: keyframes, signs and photo pins sit at a position along the track. If only single outliers were smoothed you won't notice a thing; if a lot was inserted or cut away, they can shift. When the tour changed noticeably, the program says so after the transfer.
📚 “Add this tour to the archive?” Until now a tour only entered the archive through “Folders & scan” — everything opened through a file dialog passed it by. Now the studio checks on opening: if it is exactly the archive file, nothing happens. If the tour is known but the file comes from elsewhere, you are told you are working on a copy outside the archive. And if it is unknown, you are asked — on yes the file is copied into your watched folder (the original stays put) and you continue with the archive copy. The same question follows a repair in the inspector. If you use the cloud, the tour goes up with it on its own.
  • Several ghost tracks per project — each with its own colour, width, opacity and dash pattern, reorderable by handle (the bottom one draws on top), pulled from the archive or uploaded. They are stored with the project and now show up reliably in the preview.
  • Ghost tracks from the archive can now be picked where you look for them: the selected tour offers 👻 As ghost track (a double-click does it too). Arriving from the animator's ghost button turns the archive into a pure picker — every other tool is hidden meanwhile, so a tour can't accidentally become the main track.
  • Start and finish in the inspector are clearly visible: ▶ START and ■ FINISH, with a shared marker on round trips.

v0.9.540 24 August 2026 Latest

Older cameras: the clock is not to blame.

🕐 A missing time zone is now explained. Cameras before Exif 2.31 (roughly before 2017) do not write the time zone into the photo — the clock is perfectly right, only the offset to UTC is missing. The help used to say the clock was set wrong. Now the geotagger says so itself as soon as such photos are loaded, naming the camera and the way to fix it.
🧮 The time zone is calculated, not looked up. When photos carry no time zone, the app now works it out from the track: “Computed from the track: UTC+2 — that puts 11 of 11 photos inside the track”, one click on Apply and it is set. Other programs ask a web service about the location and leave daylight saving to you. Here it is measured: no internet needed, daylight saving included automatically. And if the photos only cover one hour of a full-day tour, many time zones fit equally well — then you deliberately get no suggestion instead of a guess.
🩹 Found on the machine. The invisible cinematic flight between two merged tours still drew a black arc across the map — its shadow was not hidden along with it, in the preview and in the finished video alike. Fixed. Also: the internal transition marker showed up as a selectable data field between heart rate and cadence, and the time-zone note listed cameras that do store their time zone.
🧭 The module icons stay. When the window is too narrow for the module bar, the bar shrinks automatically. Until now the icons were the first thing to go, leaving clipped words like “Daten-Ani…”. Now it is the other way round: the small subtitle goes first, then the names are shortened, and last of all only the icons remain — the full name is in the tooltip. Widening the window brings everything back.

v0.9.532 22 August 2026

The big spring clean — every module reviewed.

🔍 Full audit. Every module was reviewed end to end — cloud, render, animator, archive, geotagger, elevation animator, inspector and maps. More than 60 findings are fixed; the most important ones are listed here.
📷 Geotagger much faster. After writing, photos are no longer re-read from disk (minutes with 5,000 images). A second drop sometimes stayed without capture time — fixed. iPhone HEICs now get the right time zone.

Fixed

  • Elevation animator did not remember the data series and locked the cancel button for the next render.
  • Web map / Tour map HTML: changing colour or width discarded your map view.
  • Inspector: “Fill gap” now asks before replacing existing points.
  • Render: map errors (e.g. invalid token) abort cleanly instead of producing black frames; GPS writing keeps foreign GPS tags such as speed.
  • Quieter log: workout files without coordinates no longer appear one by one in the log, just as one line per scan.

Many of these findings came from our own review — if you notice something, send the log, it now carries more diagnostics.
